Questions

Frequently asked

How far ahead should we book?

As early as possible, especially for the Margazhi Season. Two to three months ahead is ideal for a production.

Can a production be shorter?

Yes, most productions can be adapted to your time slot.

Live or recorded music?

Both are possible. Live orchestra details are planned with you.

Do you travel abroad?

Yes. Divyanjali has performed in the USA, Thailand, Singapore, the UK, Australia and Sri Lanka.
