The path

From first steps to arangetram

Tap a step.

Every dancer starts here: the adavus, the building blocks of Bharatanatyam, with the right posture, hands and footwork. The Nrithyaharam CD helps with practice at home.

The first short dances. Students learn to join adavus into a piece, keep the tala and perform for an audience.

The full concert repertoire, item by item, with the varnam at its heart.

Storytelling through expression, and rhythm taught as its own subject by Sri B.P. Haribabu.

When Dr. Divyasena decides a student is ready, the first full solo recital on stage. Divyanjali has conducted 71 arangetrams.

Names and durations of the levels: confirm with Dr. Divyasena

Questions

Frequently asked

What age can my child start?

From youngest age. We will suggest the right batch after a short chat.

Am I too old to learn?

No. Adults and complete beginners are welcome in the teens and adults batch.

How long until an arangetram?

It depends on the student. Most students take several years of regular classes; Dr. Divyasena decides when a student is ready.

How do online classes work across time zones?

Live classes from Chennai, with batches set at times that suit students abroad.

What do I need to buy?

Comfortable practice clothes to start. Dance sarees, salangai (ankle bells) and other items come later; we will guide you.
