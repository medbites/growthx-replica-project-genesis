Privacy

Privacy

This page explains what we collect through this website and how we use it. Have this reviewed before launch

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms: your name, WhatsApp number, email if you give it, and your message. Wishes and feedback on event pages also include your city and, if you choose, a rating.

Where it is kept

Form entries are saved in a private Google Sheet used only by the Divyanjali team. Wishes are shown on an event page only if you tick "show my message" and the team approves it.

How we use it

To reply to you, usually on WhatsApp, and to pass your wishes to the artist. We do not sell or share your details.

Videos and posts

YouTube and Instagram content loads only when you tap to play it; those services then apply their own privacy policies.

Deleting your details

Message us on WhatsApp and we will remove them.
