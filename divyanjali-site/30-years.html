2015

The flood, and the way back

In the 2015 Chennai floods the workspace stood under seven feet of water. Choreographies in digital format, library books and musical instruments, the school's treasures, were lost.

Divyanjali did not lose hope. It came back with the help of many institutions and legends: Smt. Chithra Visweswaran through ABHAI, the Music Academy, Narada Gana Sabha, Sri Nellai Kannan, Sri C.V. Chandrasekar, the core members of Prayathnam, Smt. Vasanthalakshmi Narasimhachari and Sri Jaikishore Mosalikanti, and others who asked not to be named. Today the library holds over 150 books, contributed by Smt. Nandini Ramani.

Questions

Frequently asked

When did Divyanjali complete 30 years?

In 2024. The school was founded in 1994.

What happened in the 2015 floods?

The workspace was under seven feet of water and the library, instruments and recordings were lost. Friends and institutions helped the school rebuild.

How many arangetrams has Divyanjali conducted?

Seventy-one, including one abroad. The 50th was in 2015 and the 60th in 2019.
