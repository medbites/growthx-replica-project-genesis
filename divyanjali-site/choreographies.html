Enchanting Choreographies

Festivals we host

Platforms for dance

Since 2017

Kalarpanam

A fine arts festival for young and upcoming talent, held at different venues across Chennai and its outskirts. Inaugurated by Smt. Nandini Ramani.

International

Nritya Sangamam

Solo Bharatanatyam and Kuchipudi by artists from Singapore, South Africa, the USA and Australia.

Talent search

Nrithya Saavishkar

A solo competition for ages 8 to 25, with cash prizes and a performance at a Chennai sabha for the winners.

Lecture demonstrations

Natya Satsang

Lectures, debates and quizzes with Ganamukundhapriya and Yathnam.

2019–20

Nrithya Utsav

Silver Jubilee festivals, lecture demonstrations and competitions, month on month.

Seminar

Vachika Abhinaya

A national seminar with Prayathnam, inaugurated by Padma Bhushan Dr. Padma Subrahmanyam.

Questions

Frequently asked

Can we book a production for our festival?

Yes. Send the date, venue and audience through Book and Enquire.

Can a production be shorter?

Most productions can be adapted for a shorter slot. Tell us the time you have.

Do you perform with live music?

Yes. Productions can be staged with a live orchestra or with recorded music.

What themes do you cover?

Devotional themes, Tamil literature, the epics, rhythm, tributes to composers, dance dramas and full margams.

Has Divyanjali performed abroad?

Yes: in the USA, Thailand, Singapore, the UK, Australia and Sri Lanka.

Bring a production to your stage

Festivals, temples, sabhas and cultural bodies, in India and abroad.
