Questions

Frequently asked

Can we book a production for our festival?

Yes. Send the date, venue and audience through Book and Enquire.

Can a production be shorter?

Most productions can be adapted for a shorter slot. Tell us the time you have.

Do you perform with live music?

Yes. Productions can be staged with a live orchestra or with recorded music.

What themes do you cover?

Devotional themes, Tamil literature, the epics, rhythm, tributes to composers, dance dramas and full margams.

Has Divyanjali performed abroad?

Yes: in the USA, Thailand, Singapore, the UK, Australia and Sri Lanka.

Bring a production to your stage

Festivals, temples, sabhas and cultural bodies, in India and abroad.
