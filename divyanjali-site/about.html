Divyanjali

A Bharatanatyam school in Chennai, founded by Dr. S. Divyasena. Thirty years on, it is still the same idea: teach the whole art, and make the school a second home.

Our story

Thirty years, one year at a time

Tap a year.

1994

The school begins

Dr. S. Divyasena founds Divyanjali in CIT Nagar, Chennai, to share what she had learnt from natyam with as many young minds as possible.

2004

A home of its own

Divyanjali opens its own workspace, inaugurated by Smt. Chithra Visweswaran and Smt. Nandini Ramani.

2006

The syllabus, in a book

The Divyanjali syllabus, theory and practice planned for every age group, is published as Essence and Essentials of Dance.

2011

A registered trust

Divyanjali becomes a trust. Grants from the Government of India and Tamil Isai Sangam make productions like Nava Vidha Sambandham and Andal Vaibhavam possible.

2015

The floods, and the way back

The Chennai floods hit the school hard. Students, parents and friends helped Divyanjali dance again. Read the story

2017

Kalarpanam

Divyanjali starts its own platform for young, deserving artists to perform.

2018

Sangeet International Festival

Hosted with the All India Dancers Association, with music and dance of many styles.

2019

Nrithya Utsav

For the Silver Jubilee: festivals, lecture demonstrations, workshops and competitions, month on month, from July 2019 to February 2020.

2024

Thirty years

Divyanjali completes thirty years. 71 arangetrams so far, including one abroad. Open the Souvenir 2026

What we teach

Four parts of one art

Bharatanatyam

Adavus, items and the full margam, step by step up to the arangetram.

Laya, rhythm

Tala and rhythm as a subject of its own, with Sri B.P. Haribabu.

Abhinaya

Expression and storytelling, taught by engaging children creatively.

Theory

From the school's own published syllabus, for every age group.

"Along with natyam my students learn thalam, ithihasam, puranas and moral values, to impart Poorna Vidhya. I always try to make Divyanjali their second home."Dr. S. Divyasena, Letter from the Founder

Questions

Frequently asked

When was Divyanjali founded?

In 1994, by Dr. S. Divyasena, in CIT Nagar, Chennai.

Is Divyanjali a registered trust?

Yes. Divyanjali was registered as a trust in 2011. Dr. S. Divyasena is the Founder and Managing Trustee, Sri B.P. Haribabu the Secretary-Trustee and Smt. S. Lakshmi the Treasurer-Trustee.

Who teaches the classes?

Dr. S. Divyasena leads the teaching, Sri B.P. Haribabu teaches laya, and the Divyanjali faculty, all trained at the school, teach alongside them.

Which dance form does Divyanjali teach?

Bharatanatyam, with laya, abhinaya and theory as part of every student's training.
