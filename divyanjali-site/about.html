About Divyanjali

Founded in 1994 by Dr. S. Divyasena, Divyanjali teaches Bharatanatyam with theory, rhythm and expression, and treats every student as part of the family.

Our story

From one class to a trust

Divyanjali Dance School was established in 1994 by Dr. S. Divyasena and has since trained dancers of remarkable talent, earning a place among the leading schools for Bharatanatyam in Chennai. The school opened its own workspace in 2004 in the presence of Smt. Chithra Visweswaran and Smt. Nandini Ramani.

Its syllabus, drafted by Dr. Divyasena for students of every age, covers both theory and practice and was published in 2006 as the book Essence and Essentials of Dance. Students also receive special training in laya, the rhythm of dance, from Sri B.P. Haribabu, and learn abhinaya, expression, through creative, engaging teaching.

Divyanjali was registered as a Trust in 2011 and received grants from the Government of India and the Iyal Isai Nataka Mandram, which made productions like Nava Vidha Sambandham and Andal Vaibhavam possible.

What we teach

Four parts of one art

Bharatanatyam

Adavus, items and the full margam, step by step to arangetram.

Laya, rhythm

Tala and rhythm taught as a subject of its own by Sri B.P. Haribabu.

Abhinaya

Expression and storytelling, taught creatively for every age.

Theory

Natya Shastra, mudras and rasas, from our own published syllabus.
