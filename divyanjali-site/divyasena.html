Profile

Dr. S. Divyasena is a versatile and accomplished Bharatanatyam artist, choreographer and teacher from Chennai. She trained in the preliminaries of Bharatanatyam under Smt. Saroja Rani and made her stage debut with her arangetram in 1985. She continued her rigorous training under 'Yuva Kala Bharathi' Sri J. Suryanarayana Murthy, and trained meticulously in abhinaya under 'Padma Bhushan' Smt. Kalanidhi Narayanan.

Alongside Bharatanatyam she trained in Kuchipudi for her own growth as an artist: her initiation came at the Kuchipudi Arts Academy, led by the legendary 'Padma Bhushan' Dr. Vempati Chinna Satyam, with her first years under Sri Udayakanth and Smt. Renuka, and her advanced training under Sri Jaikishore Mosalikanti. She learnt nattuvangam, the art of wielding the cymbals, from Sri B.P. Haribabu.

She is an 'A' grade artist of Doordarshan. She holds a Bachelor's in Music, a Master's in Kuchipudi and a Doctorate in Bharatanatyam from Sri Chandrasekharendra Saraswathi Viswa Mahavidyalaya, under the guidance of Dr. Ambika Kameshwar. She is also the secretary of 'Prayathnam', a forum for young classical dance teachers.

In 1994 she founded Divyanjali. Its syllabus, written by her and published as Essence and Essentials of Dance in 2006, covers theory and practice for every age group. Students also learn thalam, ithihasam, puranas and moral values, which she calls "Poorna Vidhya". Divyanjali has conducted 71 arangetrams, including one she conducted abroad. At Divyanjali she teaches Bharatanatyam.

    Frequently asked

    Who is Dr. S. Divyasena?

    A Bharatanatyam dancer, choreographer, nattuvanar and teacher from Chennai, the founder of Divyanjali (1994), a Doordarshan 'A' grade artist with a doctorate in Bharatanatyam.

    Who are her gurus?

    Smt. Saroja Rani, Sri J. Suryanarayana Murthy and, for abhinaya, Smt. Kalanidhi Narayanan. She also trained in Kuchipudi under the academy of Dr. Vempati Chinna Satyam and Sri Jaikishore Mosalikanti, and in nattuvangam under Sri B.P. Haribabu.

    What is her doctorate in?

    Bharatanatyam, from Sri Chandrasekharendra Saraswathi Viswa Mahavidyalaya, under the guidance of Dr. Ambika Kameshwar.

    Does Divyanjali teach Kuchipudi?

    No. Kuchipudi is part of her own training and informs her work; Divyanjali teaches Bharatanatyam.
