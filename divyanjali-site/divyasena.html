Dr. S. Divyasena

Dancer, choreographer and nattuvanar. Founder of Divyanjali in 1994. Doctorate in Bharatanatyam. Doordarshan A grade artist. Honoured as Nrithya Kala Sagaram.

Training

Her gurus

Dr. S. Divyasena trained in the preliminaries of Bharatanatyam under Smt. Saroja Rani and made her stage debut with her arangetram in 1985. She continued her rigorous training under 'Yuva Kala Bharathi' Sri J. Suryanarayana Murthy, and trained in abhinaya under 'Padma Bhushan' Late Smt. Kalanidhi Narayanan.

As part of her own learning she has also trained in Kuchipudi, at the Kuchipudi Arts Academy founded by 'Padma Bhushan' Dr. Vempati Chinna Satyam, and later under Sri Jai Kishore Mosalikanti. She learnt nattuvangam, the art of wielding the cymbals, from Sri B.P. Haribabu. At Divyanjali, she teaches Bharatanatyam.

Questions

Frequently asked

Where did Dr. Divyasena train?

Under Smt. Saroja Rani, Sri J. Suryanarayana Murthy and, for abhinaya, Smt. Kalanidhi Narayanan. She holds a doctorate in Bharatanatyam.

Does Divyanjali teach Kuchipudi?

No. Dr. Divyasena trained in Kuchipudi as part of her own learning, and it informs her work, but Divyanjali teaches Bharatanatyam.
