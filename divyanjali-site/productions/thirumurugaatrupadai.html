Thirumurugaatrupadai

A Sangam-era poem, staged

Plan this production

The story

What the audience sees

A famous work of Tamil literature from the Sangam era, staged as a dance production on the story behind the birth of Lord Muruga. The original work by Nakkeerar is combined with the Thirupugazh of Arunagirinadhar, which describes the six abodes (Arupadai Veedu) of Lord Muruga.
