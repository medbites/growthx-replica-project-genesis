Nava Vidha Sambandham

Nine relationships with the divine · 2014

Stage this production

The story

Kulasekara Alwar, one of the twelve Vaishnavite saints, composed ten pasurams in 'Aindhaam Thirumozhi' on the relationship between the Jeevatma and Sriman Narayana, using nine kinds of worldly relationship. Divyanjali presented them as a dance production, supported by a grant from the Ministry of Culture, Government of India, under the Production Grant Scheme.
