Andal Vaibhavam

Staged on 13 stages in one year · 2015

Stage this production

The story

A thematic production made only from the verses of Aandal (Nachiyar Thirumozhi) and a few verses of Periyalwar. It follows the life of Aandal from her birth in the thulasi garden to her union with Lord Ranganathar. Staged with a grant from the Iyal Isai Nataka Mandram, Tamil Nadu, it was presented on 13 stages across India in a single year.
