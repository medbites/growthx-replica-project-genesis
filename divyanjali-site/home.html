The guru

Dr. S. Divyasena

Founder · Natyacharya · Doordarshan A grade artist · Doctorate in Bharatanatyam

Dancer, choreographer and nattuvanar, she founded Divyanjali in 1994 and has staged more than twenty productions in India and abroad.

The musician

Sri B.P. Haribabu

Mridangam · nattuvangam · rhythm composer · Vaadhya Visharadha

Accompanying dance for more than 25 years, with over 85 tours to some 20 countries. At Divyanjali he teaches laya, rhythm, as a subject of its own.

Questions

Frequently asked

What age can my child start?

Children can start from youngest age. Adults and complete beginners of any age are welcome.

Do you teach online?

Yes. Live classes from Chennai for students abroad, with batches set to your time zone.

Do you teach Kuchipudi?

Divyanjali teaches Bharatanatyam only: technique, abhinaya, laya and theory.

Can we book a production for our festival?

Yes. Send the date, venue and audience through Book and Enquire, and we reply on WhatsApp within 24 hours.

Where is the school?

CIT Nagar, Chennai. The full address and map are on the Book and Enquire page.
