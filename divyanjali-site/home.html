Frequently asked

How do I know when the next performance is?

Every date is on the calendar. Tap Subscribe and new dates appear in your phone calendar on their own.

What age can my child start?

Children can start from youngest age. Adults and complete beginners of any age are welcome.

Do you teach online?

Yes. Live classes from Chennai for students abroad, with batches set to your time zone.

Do you teach Kuchipudi?

Divyanjali teaches Bharatanatyam only: technique, abhinaya, laya and theory.

Can we book a production for our festival?

Yes. Send the date, venue and audience through Book and Enquire, and we reply on WhatsApp within 24 hours.
