Training

Gurus and title

A versatile mridangam exponent, Sri B.P. Haribabu trained under the exemplary gurus 'Kalaimamani' Sri V.P. Ramadoss and 'Kalaimamani' Sri Thiruvaroor Bhakthavatchalam at the Tamil Nadu Government Music College. He was conferred the title 'Vaadhya Visharadha' for his mastery of the art.

Besides the mridangam, he is equally proficient in pakhawaj, nattuvangam and rhythm composition. A specialist in playing for dance concerts, he has accompanied artists for over 25 years and has worked with major dance companies in the USA.

Frequently asked

Can Sri Haribabu accompany our performance?

Yes. Send the date and venue through Book and Enquire.

Does he teach rhythm to dancers?

Yes. Laya, rhythm, is taught as a subject of its own at Divyanjali.

What is nattuvangam?

Nattuvangam is the art of conducting a Bharatanatyam performance with cymbals, reciting the rhythmic syllables that the dancer follows.
